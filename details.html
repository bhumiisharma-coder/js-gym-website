    PowerBlock Portable Weight Bench
    Convenient. Fold out the legs and perform a wide variety of exercises anywhere with PowerBlock’s compact weight bench that’s perfect for at-home or on-the-go workouts. When finished, it’s easy to slide the portable weight bench under a bed or tuck into a closet. The sturdy design makes this a reliable and versatile piece of equipment, while a durable powder-coated finish and vinyl top resist wear.
    Travel This bench is sturdier and more durable when compared to other lightweight portable benches. A bench shouldn't wobble and shake when being used. The Portable Weight Bench has a sturdy steel frame and rubber padding to prevent unnecessary movement.
    Specs

    Dimensions: 40.5″ L x 19″ W x 15″ H

    The Portable Weight Bench has foldable legs that make storage easy. Collapse the legs and store it under your bed, desk table, or in a closet.

    Weighing under 30 lbs, the Portable Weight Bench is a great bench to use when traveling. With a handle on the side frame, the bench is carriable like a suitcase.

    The Portable Weight Bench doesn't sacrifice durability for its size and weight. Thanks to its powder coat and vinyl finish, this bench can handle rigorous use.
